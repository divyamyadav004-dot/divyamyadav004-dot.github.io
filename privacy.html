Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Careerix ("we", "us") publishes free career information for students. This page explains what data we collect, how cookies are used on this site, and how third parties like Google may use cookies when ads are shown.

Information we collect

Careerix does not require you to sign up, and we do not collect your name, phone number or email through the site itself. The only information saved is stored directly in your own browser (using localStorage), not on our servers:

  • The class you selected (9, 10, 11, 12 or College), so the roadmap can hide steps you have already passed.
  • Which checklist items you have ticked on a career page.

You can clear this any time by clearing your browser's site data for Careerix.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show advertisements served by Google AdSense and other ad networks. These networks may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on someone's past visits to this website or other websites.

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area or the UK, ad networks we use may ask for your consent to use cookies for personalised ads, as required by local law.

Fonts and external resources

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on some pages, a graphics library from a content delivery network (CDN). Loading these may share your IP address and browser information with those services, in the same way it happens when visiting any website that uses them.

Children's privacy

Careerix is written for school and college students, and some visitors may be under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and we do not ask any visitor for their name, phone number, address or school details. Parents and guardians who have concerns can contact us using the details on the About page.

Links to other websites

Career pages link to official government and institute websites (such as NTA, NMC, UPSC, ICAI). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites. Please read their own privacy policies before sharing any information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as the site changes or as required by advertising partners. Please check back occasionally.

Contact

For questions about this policy, see the contact details on the About page.
